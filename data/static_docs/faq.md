# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### Q: Do I need to book in advance?
A: No, walk-ins are welcome at all locations. However, we recommend booking during peak hours (weekdays 7-10 AM) to guarantee a spot, especially at the Downtown Garage.

### Q: What are your opening hours?
A: All our parking facilities operate 24/7, 365 days a year. Customer support is available from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily.

### Q: Is my vehicle safe?
A: Yes. All facilities have 24/7 CCTV surveillance, regular security patrols, and well-lit areas. Downtown Garage and Airport Express Park also have access-controlled entry/exit gates.

### Q: Do you offer valet parking?
A: Valet service is available at Downtown Garage during weekdays (7 AM - 7 PM) for an additional $10.00 fee.

## Booking Questions

### Q: Can I extend my parking duration after I've parked?
A: Yes! Use the ParkEase app or call customer support to extend. Additional charges will apply based on standard hourly/daily rates.

### Q: What if I arrive late for my reservation?
A: Your reserved spot is held for 30 minutes past your booked start time. After that, the space may be released. If you know you'll be late, please modify your reservation.

### Q: Can I cancel my reservation?
A: Yes, cancellations are free if made at least 2 hours before the reserved start time. Late cancellations may incur a fee equal to 1 hour of parking.

### Q: Can I book for someone else?
A: Yes, just enter their vehicle license plate number during booking. The QR code can be shared with the driver.

## Payment Questions

### Q: When am I charged?
A: For reservations, you're charged upon exit (actual time used, up to your booking maximum). Monthly passes are billed at the start of each billing period.

### Q: Do you accept cash?
A: Cash is accepted at Downtown Garage only (exact change required at exit machine). All other locations are cashless.

### Q: Can I get a receipt?
A: Receipts are automatically emailed after each parking session. Corporate accounts receive monthly consolidated invoices.

### Q: What happens if I lose my QR code?
A: Your license plate is registered with your booking. Simply enter your plate at the gate, or call support for assistance.

## Technical Questions

### Q: What if the gate doesn't open?
A: Press the intercom button at the gate for immediate assistance from our control room (available 24/7).

### Q: Do you have EV charging?
A: Yes, Downtown Garage has 20 EV charging stations (Level 2 and DC fast charge). Book with "EV" vehicle type to guarantee charger access.

### Q: Is there WiFi in the parking garages?
A: Free WiFi is available in Downtown Garage and Airport Express Park common areas (elevators, stairwells, walkways).

### Q: What if my vehicle is damaged while parked?
A: Report any damage to customer support immediately. Our CCTV footage will be reviewed. ParkEase is not liable for vehicle damage unless caused by our equipment or staff negligence, per our terms of service.
