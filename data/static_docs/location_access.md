# Location & Access Guide

## Downtown Garage

### Address
123 Main Street, City Center, 10001

### Getting There
- **By car**: From Highway 101, take Exit 12 (City Center). Turn right on Main Street. Garage entrance is on the left after 200 meters.
- **Public transit**: Bus routes 4, 7, 15 stop at "Main Street / Commerce" (50m walk). Metro Blue Line - "City Center" station (2 min walk).
- **Landmarks nearby**: City Hall (100m), Central Library (200m), Commerce Mall (50m)

### Entrance/Exit
- **Vehicle entrance**: Main Street (south side)
- **Vehicle exit**: Commerce Lane (east side, one-way)
- **Pedestrian exits**: Main Street (ground floor), Commerce Lane (all floors via stairwell)
- **Height clearance**: 2.1 meters (6'10") all levels

### Accessibility
- Disabled parking: 15 spaces on ground floor near elevator
- Elevator access to all floors
- Wide pedestrian pathways
- Braille signage on all levels

## Riverside Lot

### Address
45 River Road, Waterfront District, 10005

### Getting There
- **By car**: From Ring Road, take the Waterfront exit. Follow River Road south for 500m. Lot entrance is on the right.
- **Public transit**: Bus route 22 stops at "River Road / Pier" (directly adjacent). Ferry terminal 5 min walk.
- **Landmarks nearby**: Waterfront Promenade (adjacent), Riverside Market (100m), Ferry Terminal (300m)

### Entrance/Exit
- **Single entrance/exit**: River Road (barrier-controlled)
- **Height clearance**: No restriction (open air)

### Accessibility
- Flat lot, fully accessible
- Marked disabled spaces near the entrance (8 spaces)
- No stairs required

---

## Airport Express Park

### Address
789 Terminal Way, Airport Zone, 10099

### Getting There
- **By car**: Follow Airport Highway signs to "Long-Term Parking / Express Park". Entrance is clearly signposted 500m before the terminal.
- **Shuttle**: Free shuttle bus runs between the lot and Terminals 1, 2, and 3 every 10 minutes (5:00 AM - 1:00 AM). On-demand shuttle available 1 AM - 5 AM (press call button at shuttle stop).
- **From city center**: 25 minutes via Highway 101 (no traffic), 40-60 minutes during peak hours.

### Entrance/Exit
- **Vehicle entrance**: Terminal Way (north side)
- **Vehicle exit**: Terminal Way (south side)
- **Height clearance**: 2.4 meters (7'10") all covered levels; unlimited on rooftop level

### Accessibility
- Disabled parking: 25 spaces on Level 1 near shuttle stop
- Elevators at each stairwell core
- Shuttle buses are wheelchair accessible
- Luggage trolleys available at shuttle drop-off points
