# ParkEase - Smart Parking Solutions

## About Us

ParkEase is a modern parking management company operating premium parking facilities across the city center. We provide secure, convenient, and affordable parking solutions for daily commuters, shoppers, and event attendees.

## Our Facilities

We operate three parking locations:

### Downtown Garage
- **Address**: 123 Main Street, City Center
- **Capacity**: 500 spaces (including 20 EV charging stations)
- **Floors**: 5 levels (B1, G, 1, 2, 3)
- **Features**: 24/7 security cameras, well-lit, elevator access, disabled parking on ground floor

### Riverside Lot
- **Address**: 45 River Road, Waterfront District
- **Capacity**: 200 spaces (open-air)
- **Features**: Direct river walk access, bicycle parking, motorcycle section

### Airport Express Park
- **Address**: 789 Terminal Way, Airport Zone
- **Capacity**: 800 spaces (covered structure)
- **Features**: Free shuttle to terminals every 10 minutes, luggage trolleys, long-term storage lockers
