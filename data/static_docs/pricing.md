## Discounts & Promotions

- **Early Bird Special**: Arrive before 7:00 AM at Downtown Garage → flat rate $10.00/day
- **Weekend Special**: Saturday & Sunday at Riverside Lot → flat rate $8.00/day
- **Loyalty Program**: Every 10th day free for monthly pass holders
- **Student Discount**: 20% off monthly passes with valid student ID
- **Corporate Accounts**: Custom rates available for companies with 5+ employees parking regularly

## Payment Methods

- Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Mobile payment (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- ParkEase app wallet
- Cash (Downtown Garage only, exact change)
- Monthly invoicing for corporate accounts
