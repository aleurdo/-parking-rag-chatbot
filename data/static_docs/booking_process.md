# Booking & Reservation Process

## How to Reserve a Parking Spot

### Online Booking (Recommended)
1. Visit our website or open the ParkEase app
2. Select your preferred location (Downtown Garage, Riverside Lot, or Airport Express Park)
3. Choose your date and time of arrival
4. Select your departure date/time (or choose open-ended)
5. Pick your vehicle type (standard, EV, motorcycle)
6. Confirm your booking and receive a QR code

### Phone Booking
Call +1 (555) 123-4567 during support hours (6 AM - 11 PM) to book with an agent.

### Walk-in
No reservation needed — simply drive in if spaces are available. However, we cannot guarantee availability without a reservation, especially during peak hours.

## Reservation Rules

- **Advance booking**: Reservations can be made up to 30 days in advance
- **Minimum duration**: 1 hour
- **Maximum duration**: 30 days (contact us for longer stays)
- **Cancellation policy**: Free cancellation up to 2 hours before reserved start time
- **Late arrival**: Your spot is held for 30 minutes past your reserved start time, then released
- **Modification**: Reservations can be modified up to 1 hour before start time at no extra cost

## Required Information for Booking

- Full name
- Email address (for confirmation)
- Vehicle license plate number
- Preferred location
- Date and time (arrival and optional departure)
- Vehicle type (standard, EV, motorcycle)

## Check-in Process

1. Drive to the entrance gate
2. Scan your QR code or enter your license plate
3. Gate opens automatically
4. Park in any available space on your assigned level (or any open space for Riverside Lot)

## Check-out Process

1. Return to your vehicle
2. Drive to the exit gate
3. Scan your QR code or enter your license plate
4. Payment is automatically processed (or confirmed as pre-paid)
5. Gate opens — drive out safely

## Special Requests

- **Disabled parking**: Request ground-floor allocation at Downtown Garage
- **EV charging**: Specify EV during booking to guarantee charger access
- **Oversized vehicles**: Vans and trucks must book at Riverside Lot (extra wide spaces)
